Gas turbine development from micro-turbines up to 75 MW

We develop gas turbine units and individual components under design and development contracts, taking a project from the technical specification and conceptual design through detailed documentation and testing to production start-up at the partner's plant.

Track record

The team has taken 10 MW and 40 MW machines through to testing

A 40 MW industrial gas turbine developed from a clean sheet. The analysis and design documentation were completed in 12 months; full-scale testing of the first prototype ended in late 2024 with 40.26 MW at 41.26% simple-cycle efficiency (ISO conditions), above the design targets.

The team's competence covers the range from micro-turbines up to 75 MW: simple and recuperated cycles, single-shaft and two-shaft layouts with a free power turbine, machines for power generation and mechanical drive.

Disciplines

Every part of the machine is handled by a dedicated specialist

Flow path

Thermodynamic cycle, compressor and turbine aerodynamics, numerical flow simulation. Output: flow path geometry and machine performance across the whole operating range.

Combustor

Combustor layout and design, combustion and emissions calculation, burner selection for natural gas, hydrogen blends and low-calorific-value gases.

Cooling

Blade and disc cooling systems, air distribution through the secondary circuits, part temperatures as input for strength analysis.

Strength and life

Stress and strain in discs, blades and casings, vibration and rotor dynamics, setting the service life and overhaul intervals.

Design and documentation

Unit arrangement, 3D models and complete design, manufacturing and repair documentation.

Materials and suppliers

Selection of materials and coatings, requirements for casting and machining, supplier qualification for the partner.

Testing

Test programmes, participation in rig and full-scale tests, processing of results and design updates.

Compressor and auxiliary machinery

Axial and centrifugal compressors and auxiliary equipment, matched to the main machine.

Workflow

From specification to series production support

  1. Technical specification

    Application, target parameters, fuel, site conditions, emission and service life requirements.

  2. Conceptual design

    Cycle and layout selection, preliminary arrangement, performance and cost estimates.

  3. Detailed design

    Full scope of analysis, 3D models, release of design documentation.

  4. Production start-up

    Manufacturing technology, tooling, supplier qualification, design supervision at the partner's plant.

  5. Testing and development

    Test programme, rig and full-scale tests, documentation updates based on the results.

  6. Series support

    Design changes, operating and repair documentation, service support.

In the micro-turbine class, operation on natural gas, biogas, diesel fuel and hydrogen blends has been demonstrated with NOx emissions below 15 ppm (at 15% O₂, dry).

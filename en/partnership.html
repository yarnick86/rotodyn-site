Ways of working and the partnership model

We work in several formats, from a one-off engineering analysis to a joint programme for the development and localised production of gas turbines at the partner's plant.

Advisory, supply, market analysis

Advisory and transactions

Joint venture support, technical and commercial due diligence of counterparties, negotiation support in Russian, English and Chinese, structuring of agreements.

Market analysis

Gas turbine market reviews, platform comparisons for a specific task, technical checks of equipment before a transaction.

Joint development and localisation

The partner company owns the plant, the rights and the jobs

The investment goes into a company established in the partner's country. ROTODYN acts as its engineering and design centre and transfers the design, documentation and test data under contract.

Programme

Three phases over 42 months

An indicative plan for a joint programme to develop and locally produce a mid-range machine based on a tested gas generator. The order of the phases may be changed by agreement with the partner.

PhaseScopeOutcome
Phase 1 · months 0–20Natural gas baseline, adaptation to the ambient and grid conditions of the target market, localisation planValidated configuration for the target market

Getting started

Four steps to the main contract

  1. Problem definition

    Free of charge. One or two calls to understand the subject and the constraints.

  2. Technical proposal

    Scope, schedule, cost, team composition and list of deliverables.

  3. Pilot project

    A small paid scope, such as a calculation, inspection or technical opinion, to test how we work together.

  4. Main contract

    The full scope of work, with methods and pace clear to both sides.

Geography: Russia and the CIS, Russian–Chinese projects; international programmes in the Middle East, South-East and South Asia, Latin America and Africa.

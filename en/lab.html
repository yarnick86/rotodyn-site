AI-assisted engineering modelling

A line of work that combines the team's calculation methods with machine learning tools. The aim is to shorten the time needed for analysis and documentation without loss of quality. The model proposes; the engineer checks and signs off.

Services

What ROTODYN LAB does

01

Analysis reports

Aerodynamics, 3D CFD, thermal state, strength and life. Each report states the methodology, input data, assumptions and limits of applicability.

02

Platform comparison

Parametric selection of a machine for the task: efficiency at real operating points, service life, fuel and ownership costs, delivery time.

03

Automated calculation chains

Linking 1D design, 3D analysis and strength calculations into a single workflow that recalculates when the input data change.

04

Automated documentation release

Templates, completeness and consistency checks, generation of bills of materials.

05

Digital twin

A model of the machine tuned to its own operating data. It helps spot deviations earlier, estimate the remaining life and plan repairs by condition.

06

Monitoring data analysis

Trend analysis of operating parameters for service companies and operators, input to repair decisions.

Principle

The engineer is responsible for the conclusion

AI tools speed up routine steps: preparing calculation models, working through design options, checking documents. The methodology, assumptions and final conclusions stay with specialists who have designed and tested gas turbines.

Pilot project: one calculation based on the customer's data, for example a residual life assessment of a stage from a completed inspection, or a comparison of two machines for a specific site.

Tell us about your project

The first conversation is free of charge: one or two calls to understand the subject and the constraints. Then a technical proposal with scope, schedule and cost.

An independent full-cycle engineering organisation

ROTODYN designs, analyses and tests gas turbine equipment and hands the documentation over to its partners. We are not a manufacturer defending its own platform: production stays with the partner who builds the machine.

The name

Rotor and dynamics

The name ROTODYN combines the words rotor and dynamics. It comes from the teams that designed gas turbine units from micro-turbines up to 75 MW, analysed them and brought them to maturity on test benches.

In Russia the company works under the name Ротодин.

Experience

Behind the numbers

  • Chief designer: Doctor of Technical Sciences and Professor. Headed strength and design departments in the gas turbine engine industry; author of a monograph, 96 papers and 8 patents.
  • Team experience: steam turbines from 6 to 1,200 MW and gas turbines up to 400 MW; participation in launching series production of single-crystal blades with thermal barrier coatings.
  • 14 specialists have more than 20 years' experience. A project team is formed for each contract from an extended pool of more than 70 engineers and specialists.
  • Our specialists' backgrounds: Russian engine manufacturers, GE, Siemens, Alstom, Ansaldo Energia, ABB, Pratt & Whitney, Nuovo Pignone.
